## Research idea

Universal quantum cloning becomes less effective as the dimension of the state space grows. The paper introduces a Hybrid Quantum Autocloning Machine that:

1. compresses a high-dimensional quantum state into a lower-dimensional effective subspace with a quantum autoencoder;
2. performs universal quantum cloning in that reduced space; and
3. reconstructs the cloned states in the original Hilbert space.

For states with strong overlap with the effective qubit subspace, the method exceeds the benchmark fidelity of direct universal qutrit cloning, approaches the optimal qubit-cloning limit, and remains robust under noise.
