## Context

As a research engineer at A\*STAR's Singapore Institute of Manufacturing Technology (SIMTech), I work on machine-learning methods for industrial time-series and predictive-maintenance problems.

## Current work

- Adapt time-series foundation models for fault detection and tool-wear prediction.
- Build reproducible fine-tuning and evaluation workflows for large-scale experiments on HPC infrastructure.
- Explore generative modelling for industrial data, particularly settings where anomalous examples are limited.

## Conference activity

I co-authored the accepted CLF 2026 abstract “From Signals to Decisions: A Pedagogical Pipeline for Tool-Wear Prediction Using Few-Shot Learning” and attended the 16th Conference on Learning Factories in Bolzano, Italy.
