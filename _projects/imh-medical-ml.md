## Context

During my machine-learning internship at Singapore's Institute of Mental Health, I worked on computational methods for medical prediction and diagnostic decision support.

## My work

- Developed a generative modelling pipeline based on a variational autoencoder, improving prediction accuracy by 20% over baseline models.
- Used Ray on a high-performance computing cluster to parallelise hyperparameter tuning.
- Built [nPipeline](https://github.com/01QJ10/nPipeline), which uses language embeddings and large language models to connect patient nutrients data with relevant studies in a genetic database.
- Developed computational methods for selecting efficient sequences of diagnostic tests.

The VAE work was showcased at the Singapore Health and Biomedical Congress 2024 as “A Genetic Approach to Predicting Nutrient Deficiencies Using Variational Autoencoder (VAE).” Patient data and non-public implementation details are intentionally omitted.
